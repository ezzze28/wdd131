Volt Panther Radio Site Plan

Ezequiel Nicolidakis - WDD 131 - Project

Site Name

Volt Panther Radio

Reason: This name represents a high-energy, independent web radio station. Combining "Volt" and "Panther" reflects a sleek, electrifying, and modern identity that perfectly matches the site's vibrant color scheme and alternative music focus.

Site Purpose

Volt Panther Radio is built for anyone who wants to stay on top of the world's biggest music hits and trending charts. Whether you're browsing what's dominating the weekly countdown, listening to quick audio previews, or sending in your own song requests, it’s a high-energy hub designed to keep you connected to the tracks you love.

Scenarios

Scenario 1: How can I listen to previews of this week's top artists on the charts?

Scenario 2: Where can I submit a request for a song to be played on the station, or suggest a new artist?

Scenario 3: How can I filter the weekly charts to find artists of a specific genre?

Color Schema

  • #2B2Df5  Primary - Background Color
  • #0081C9  Secondary - Complementary to the primary and also adds variation
  • #EDF126  Accent 1 - Most of the text
  • #361C18  Accent 2 - Dark accent

Typography

I selected two different typography to integrate into the site. Syncopate is for the titles and Source Sans 3 for the content.
